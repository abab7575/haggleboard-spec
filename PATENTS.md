Patent non-assert. The HaggleBoard project promises not to assert any patent claim it
owns or controls that is necessarily infringed by implementing the HaggleBoard
specification (SPEC.md and schemas/), against anyone implementing it, royalty-free, worldwide, and
irrevocably, except against a party that asserts a patent claim against any
implementation of this specification. Contributors to the specification agree to the same.
This is modelled on the Open Web Foundation Final Specification Agreement (OWFa 1.0).
